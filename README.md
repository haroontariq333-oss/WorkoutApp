# Overload

A single-page, no-install workout app built around evidence-based hypertrophy principles: progressive overload, effort measured in reps in reserve (RIR), 10–20 weekly hard sets per muscle, and protein-first nutrition.

Open `index.html` in any browser. It needs no build step and no server. Data is saved in the browser's local storage.

## Features

- **Plan**: builds a program from your goal (lose fat, build muscle, recomp), experience (beginner or intermediate), equipment (full gym, dumbbells, bodyweight) and schedule (3, 4 or 5 days). It shows sets, rep ranges, RIR and rest times, plus a weekly-volume chart for each muscle.
- **Train**: logs sets with a rest timer. Weights are suggested by double progression: once every set reaches the top of the rep range, the app tells you to add weight.
- **Progress**: logged sets per muscle over the last 7 days, body-weight trend with a 7-day average, estimated 1RM trend for each exercise, and workout history.
- **Nutrition**: calories and macros from the Mifflin–St Jeor equation, adjusted for your goal.
- **Exercises**: 40 exercises with form cues, searchable and filterable by muscle.
- kg/lb units, plus backup and restore through copy and paste.
