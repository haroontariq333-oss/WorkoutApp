Privacy policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

Overload is a workout and nutrition app run by {{OPERATOR}} at {{APP_URL}}. This policy explains what information the app collects, why, who it's shared with, and how you can see or delete it. Questions go to {{CONTACT}}.

You can use Overload without an account. Then everything you enter stays on your device and nothing is sent to us.

What we collect

Only when you create an account:

InformationWhy
Email addressTo sign you in, confirm the account is yours, and send password reset links.
PasswordTo sign you in. We store only a scrambled form (an scrypt hash), never the password itself.
Workouts, sets, weights and repsTo sync your training between your devices and show your progress.
Body weight entries, age, sex, height and activity levelTo show your weight trend and calculate calorie and protein targets.
Program and app settings, such as goal, equipment, units and dietTo keep the app set up the same way on every device.
Reminder days, time and time zone, and a push notification address for each deviceTo send workout reminders, only if you turn them on.
IP addressKept briefly in memory to block repeated failed sign-in attempts. Our hosting provider may also keep it in standard server logs.

Body weight and fitness details can count as health information in some countries. We use it only to run the app for you.

What we don't do

  • We don't sell your information or share it with advertisers.
  • We don't show ads or use analytics or tracking tools.
  • We use one cookie, which keeps you signed in. It isn't used for tracking.

Who else handles your data

We use a few services to run the app. They process data only on our behalf:

  • Hosting provider: stores the account database and runs the server.
  • Email provider: delivers confirmation and password reset emails. It receives your email address and the message.
  • Your browser's push service (Google, Apple, Mozilla or Microsoft, depending on your device): delivers reminders. Reminder messages are encrypted, so the push service can't read them.
  • Google Fonts: provides the app's typefaces. Your browser downloads them directly from Google.

We may disclose information if the law requires it.

Legal basis

If you're in the EU or UK: we process your account information to provide the service you signed up for, and your fitness and body details with your consent, which you give when you create an account and can withdraw at any time by deleting your account.

How long we keep it

We keep your information while you have an account. When you delete your account, your account information, workouts, weigh-ins, settings and reminders are removed from the database immediately. Copies in our hosting provider's backups are overwritten within about 30 days.

Your choices and rights

  • See and copy your data: in the app, open Plan, then Settings, and choose Copy backup. It contains everything we store about your training.
  • Correct it: remove a workout from Progress, then History; log a new weigh-in to replace that day's entry; and change your details in the Nutrition tab.
  • Delete everything: open Plan, then Settings, and choose Delete account.
  • Stop reminders: turn them off in Settings or block notifications for the app on your device.

Depending on where you live, you may have other rights, such as objecting to processing or complaining to your data protection authority. Contact us at {{CONTACT}} and we'll respond within 30 days.

Security

Data travels over encrypted HTTPS connections. Passwords are hashed, sign-in sessions use random tokens stored only in hashed form, and repeated sign-in attempts are blocked. No system is perfectly secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

Children

Overload isn't meant for children under 16, and we don't knowingly collect their information. If you believe a child has created an account, contact us and we'll delete it.

Not medical advice

Training plans and nutrition targets in the app are general fitness information, not medical advice. Check with a doctor before starting a new exercise or diet plan if you have health concerns.

Changes

If we change this policy, we'll update the date above. For significant changes, we'll let account holders know in the app or by email.
